## 💡 **WHY NO GREY BOX WITH SVG:**

**SVG (Scalable Vector Graphics):**
- ✅ **Built-in transparency** - Background never renders
- ✅ **Vector paths** - No pixel background
- ✅ **Browser native** - Rendered as pure graphics
- ✅ **No image format issues** - Not JPEG/PNG compression
- ✅ **CSS friendly** - Easy to style and filter

**Result:** Impossible to have grey box! 🎉
